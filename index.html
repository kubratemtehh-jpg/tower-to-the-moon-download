How it plays

DROP

Tap the middle on the boom

The hanging block swings with the music. Tick, tick, tick, boom: on the boom it is right above the tower.

FIX

Tap a side to swing the hammer

Nothing ever falls, but a crooked tower bends and locks. Tap left or right of a block and the hammer knocks it back. Hold to bolt on a brace.

SPEED 1–100

Stay in sync and it speeds up

Land within 10% of centre twice in a row and the tempo climbs. The faster you go, the more the block is pulled toward the centre, up to halfway at speed 100. Rest and it slows down, and every new layer of the sky starts again at speed 1.

SPOT

Tap what flies past

Kites, a witch on a broom, a winged horse, a dragon, the Simurgh, airliners, the space station, meteors, UFOs, Russell's teapot and the flying spaghetti. Tap them as they pass in front of the tower for mortar.
