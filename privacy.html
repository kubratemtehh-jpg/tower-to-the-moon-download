Privacy Policy for Tower to the Moon

Last updated: 10 October 2026

Tower to the Moon does not collect, store, share or sell any personal information. The game has no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no third-party tracking.

Your game progress (tower height, upgrades and settings) is saved only on your own device. Uninstalling the game or clearing its data deletes it.

The game does not request access to your location, contacts, camera, microphone or files. It uses vibration for short haptic feedback.

The game is suitable for all ages and does not knowingly collect information from children.

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page.

Questions: open an issue at github.com/kubratemtehh-jpg/tower-to-the-moon-download/issues.
